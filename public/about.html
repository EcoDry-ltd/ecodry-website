About EcoDry

Helping good produce go further.

EcoDry focuses on practical post-harvest preservation through hybrid solar dryers, dried agricultural products and drying-as-a-service.

The challenge we care about

For farmers, processors and food businesses, a successful harvest can also create pressure: fresh produce may spoil before it can be sold, processed or stored. Drying is one approach that can help reduce moisture and create more flexibility when it is done safely and appropriately.

EcoDry brings together equipment, products and service options so customers can explore an approach that fits their needs.

Our focus

Practical, accessible preservation.

  • Hybrid solar dryer solutions
  • Dried produce and processed ingredients
  • Drying service for customer-supplied produce
  • Education around preservation and post-harvest handling
