Before you order

Delivery and payment, clearly explained.

We confirm product availability, delivery options and the final amount with you before dispatch. This page will be updated when EcoDry's BACHS payment integration is ready.

Delivery

  • Availability is confirmed before an order is accepted.
  • Delivery location, cost and estimated timeline are agreed with the customer.
  • For drying services, produce acceptance, preparation and drop-off arrangements must be confirmed first.
  • Keep your order reference and proof of payment for follow-up.

Payment

EcoDry plans to use BACHS for online payment. A secure live checkout cannot be enabled until the official BACHS merchant documentation, sandbox credentials and callback verification details are configured and tested.

Current status: Orders and inquiries are handled manually. Do not treat an order inquiry as a paid order, and do not send payment to an unverified account.
Ask about an order

Order confirmation

Submitting a product inquiry does not reserve stock or confirm a sale. EcoDry will confirm availability, quantity, total cost, delivery and payment instructions with you.

Returns and issues

If an order arrives damaged, incorrect or incomplete, contact EcoDry promptly with your order details and relevant photos so the team can review the issue. Final return, replacement and refund terms should be confirmed and published before public launch.
